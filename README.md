## CS 230 Operating Platforms

### Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?

The Gaming Room was the client for this project. They wanted to expand their game, Draw It or Lose It, into a web-based application that could work on different operating platforms. The application needed to support multiple teams and players while making sure names were unique.

### What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?

I think I did well comparing the different operating platforms and explaining which option would work best for the client. I also considered important areas such as security, storage, performance, and scalability.

### What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?

The design document helped me understand the requirements before working on the code. It gave me a better idea of how the different parts of the application should work together and helped keep the development process organized.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

I would improve the recommendations section. I would add more detail about why certain platforms and technologies would be better choices and provide stronger comparisons between the available options.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I focused on the client's need for a game that could support multiple users and work across different platforms. I also considered security and scalability when designing the application. Considering user needs is important because the final software needs to solve the client's problems and be easy for users to access.

### How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?

I started by understanding the requirements and then broke the application into smaller parts. I compared different platforms and considered which solution would best meet the client's needs. In the future, I would continue planning the software before coding, comparing different solutions, and breaking larger requirements into smaller tasks.
